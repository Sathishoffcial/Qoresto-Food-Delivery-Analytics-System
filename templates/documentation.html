QORESTO Restaurant Intelligence System

Food Ordering • Analytics • Business Intelligence Platform

📖 Project Overview

QORESTO is a full-stack restaurant intelligence and food ordering platform built using Flask and MySQL. The system allows customers to browse restaurants, place orders, track deliveries, manage favorites, submit reviews, and provides advanced analytics for restaurant administrators.

⚙️ Core Modules

  • Restaurant Management
  • User Management
  • Menu Management
  • Order Processing
  • Payment Tracking
  • Reviews & Ratings
  • Favorites Management
  • Analytics Dashboard
  • Reports & Export System
